Where we are today

9,193 products across 7 brands in Neon. The infrastructure is mature — B2 document storage, Neon database, Shopify metafield pipeline, and 5 of 7 brands have push scripts. But coverage is uneven: specs range from 42% (Votatec) to 0% (Eiko, Eurofase, Turolight), documents from 46% (Canolight) to 98% (Eiko), and 1,371 products still haven't been pushed to Shopify.

This is a data-quality project, not an architecture project. The pipelines exist. What's missing is systematic execution.
